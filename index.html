About us

A landscape design-build company based in Oakville

Couture Landscapes is an experienced residential and commercial landscaping company with a commitment to excellence, workmanship and craftsmanship. Our owner, Rob Couture, along with a committed and dedicated crew, has built a business that specializes in landscape design, construction and outdoor living spaces.

We've been a staple in the Burlington, Oakville and Mississauga area for over fifteen years. From the beginning of each project our focus is meeting your needs in ways that are practical and beautiful.

Our company name has been updated to Couture Group. Same owner, same crew, same commitment to our clients.

Landscape services

Everything outside, designed and built

Landscape design and construction in Burlington, Oakville, Halton, Mississauga and nearby areas. Tap any service for details.

🏡Backyard LandscapingResort-style backyards, made for living

We help homeowners bring the ambiance of their favourite resorts and getaways right to their backyard, turning dull open areas into comfortable, memorable and functional outdoor living spaces. We work one-on-one with you to understand your needs, then combine our ideas into a design that fits the feel you want.

🌻Front Yard LandscapingCurb appeal and an elegant entrance

Complete front yard landscaping that complements your home's architecture: interlocking walkways lined with plants and greenery or a more formal look, plus fencing, gardening, planting, front porch and accent features.

🏀Backyard Sports CourtsBasketball, tennis & pickleball

Custom-designed backyard basketball, tennis and pickleball courts. We work with you on court dimensions, surface materials and colours. Our premium surfaces are durable, low-maintenance and built to handle Canadian weather, and pickleball courts are designed to official standards.

🚗Interlocking DrivewaysDurable, upscale and fully sealed

Quality interlocking stone for driveways, patios and pathways. We bring samples so you can compare styles against your garage doors, brick colour and landscape, handle the proper pre-construction work, and finish every driveway with complete sealing.

🧱Interlocking WalkwaysBuilt to code, finished cleanly

We start by assessing the area, then compare design and colour samples with your existing landscape. Our crew follows municipal and building codes for structural integrity, values your input throughout, and leaves a pristine cleanup at the end.

💧Irrigation & DrainageAutomatic sprinklers and yard drainage

Automatic irrigation systems that save time, money and water. Every lawn is different, so we design each system around the needs of your plantings, from small to large installations, along with yard drainage solutions.

🚧Landscape ConstructionPatios, decks, fences & stonework
  • Fences, patios, decks, gazebos and sheds
  • Complete landscape design + build
  • Walkways, retaining walls, stonework, flagstone and interlocking stone
  • Pond and waterfall landscaping
  • Outdoor kitchens and custom wood structures
🔥Outdoor Living SpacesPrivate spaces for entertaining

Outdoor living areas built with the masonry, drainage, compaction and structural know-how they need. We coordinate with municipal codes, building permits and city regulations, and can carry related work like irrigation and electrical.

🏊Swimming Pool LandscapingPool surrounds, ponds & waterfalls

Whether you have an in-ground or above-ground pool, we create a livable, resort-style space around it with interlocking stone, greenery, waterfalls and ponds, using high-grade materials and working one-on-one with you throughout.

🪨Retaining WallsConcrete, stone, brick or wood

Retaining walls that hold back soil and tame slopes, staircases and changes in elevation while looking great. We build in concrete, wood, stone or brick, including cantilevered walls with steel supports where extra strength is needed.

🌳Gardens & PlantingTrees, shrubs and colourful beds

Custom garden and tree planting designs. Careful handling and proper planting technique are critical to a tree's growth, so our crew is trained to do it right. The service includes planting, watering and cleanup.

💡Landscape LightingBeauty and safety after dark

From small LED accent lights to post cap lighting, we light gardens, entrances, patios, lawns and water features, highlighting your home's architecture and making paths safer for guests.
